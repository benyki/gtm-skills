# TikTok Descriptions (captions) for your project videos

Ready-to-use caption examples for your project TikTok posts. Use these as the `--caption`
text.

> ⚠️ **Match the caption to the video.** Some captions below only make sense for a
> specific *type* of video (e.g. a video about reading books). **Always pick — or adapt —
> a caption that actually fits what's on screen.** Don't slap a "books" caption on a
> lockscreen video, or a generic one where a specific angle would land better. When in
> doubt, rewrite to match the footage. Keep the CTA + `your-site.example` link.

The link is always **your-site.example**, and the offer is that your-app is **free** to start.

---

## Lockscreen videos — use ONLY these

For the lock-screen promo videos (`lockscreen-fr`, `lockscreen-es`, `lockscreen-it`),
use **only** the four captions below. Pick one and substitute `<language>` (e.g. `French`).
Do **not** use the Generic or Books captions on a lockscreen video.

- ```
  It is the translation app I use now. The words you translate go into your collection automatically, and you end up learning <language> just by looking at your screen. Tons of languages supported.
  ```

- ```
  Translator that remembers every word for you and help you memorize them. Mine's basically a <language> vocab list I never had to make.
  ```

- ```
  It's a translation app, but the words don't disappear after you look them up — they get saved to your library. So the stuff you actually run into day to day becomes the stuff you learn.
  ```

- ```
  You know how you translate a word and forget it ten seconds later? This one just keeps them. Every word lands in your library, and after a while you realize you've actually learned a lot
  ```

## Generic — works for any your-app video

Use these when the video is about collecting words / learning a language in general
(lockscreen, vocab, definition, slang, POV videos, etc.).

  Start your your-app for free: your-site.example

- ```

  ```

  ```

- ```

  ```
