# Credits

This skill is a synthesis of other people's work. Nothing in it was discovered
here — the platform rankings, the directory lists and the ordering principle all
come from founders who ran the launches and posted what happened.

If you use this skill and it works, the people below are why. Go follow them.

## What isn't credited

The per-platform mechanics — Product Hunt's midnight PT window, Hacker News's
Show HN rules and 30-minute window, the Reddit self-promo ratio, the lifetime-deal
warning, all launch-day timing — are **general practice, not from these sources**.
`references/platforms.md` marks the boundary with `[E]` / `[H]` tags, and every
platform section tells you to verify against the platform's own rules page before
submitting. Where this skill is confident and the sources are silent, that's on
this skill, not on them.
