# Show HN

The hardest format on the list, because everything that works elsewhere fails here.
Read Hacker News's own Show HN rules page before submitting — it's short and it's
enforced.

**Precondition:** people can try it right now, without a waitlist and without a
sales call. If they can't, this isn't a Show HN.

---

## Title

```
Show HN: <what it is, plainly>
```

- ✅ `Show HN: A CLI that submits your product to 100 launch directories`
- ❌ `Show HN: The revolutionary way to 10x your launch 🚀`

No emoji. No superlatives. No question marks. The title is a description, not a
hook — on HN the hook *is* the plainness.

---

## The post

Link to the thing itself, not to a landing page about the thing. If you must link
to a landing page, it should show the product above the fold with no signup gate.

---

## First comment — post it right after submitting

```
Author here. <One sentence on what it does.>

I built this because <the actual problem, specific, from your own experience>.

How it works: <the technical approach in 2–3 sentences. Name the real stack. This
is the part HN reads.>

What's hard about it: <the genuinely tricky part>.

Current limitations: <2–3 real ones>.

<Stack / open source status / pricing — plainly.>

Happy to answer questions.
```

**Lead with the mechanism.** This audience wants to know *how*, and rewards a
straight answer more than any benefit claim.

---

## Rules that are actually enforced

- **No marketing language.** "Revolutionary", "seamless", "game-changing", "supercharge" — every one of these costs you credibility on first read
- **Never organise upvotes.** Voting rings get accounts *and domains* banned. Not a grey area
- **Don't argue with critics.** "Fair point, that's a real limitation" is the highest-scoring reply on HN. Defensiveness kills threads
- **Don't edit the post to add promotion later**

## Timing

Weekday mornings US Eastern for maximum traffic and maximum competition. Weekends
are quieter on both — sometimes the right trade for a small project.

The first **20–30 minutes** decide it. If it doesn't move off `/newest`, it's over.
That's the normal outcome and it isn't a verdict on the product — resubmitting a
genuinely improved version months later is acceptable; resubmitting the same thing
next week is not.

## Expect

Blunt technical criticism, and at least one comment saying this could be a shell
script. Answering that one well is worth more traffic than the post.
