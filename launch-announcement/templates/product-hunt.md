```
Hi PH 👋

What it does:
- <capability, concrete>
- <capability, concrete>
- <capability, concrete>

What it doesn't do yet: <the honest gap>.

Pricing: <exact — free tier, paid tier, numbers>.

<One open question for the community — something you actually want an answer to.>

```

The "doesn't do yet" line is not modesty. It's the line that makes the rest
credible.

## Prepared answers

Write these before launch day. You will be asked all three.

- **"How is this different from `<obvious competitor>`?"** — one sentence, no disparaging
- **"Is this just a wrapper around `<model/API>`?"** — say what you built and what you didn't
- **"Why is it priced at X?"** — one sentence, no apologising

## Launch day checklist

- [ ] Scheduled for 12:01 AM PT (confirm PH's current scheduling rules first)
- [ ] Tue–Thu unless deliberately picking a quiet day
- [ ] First comment posted within minutes of going live
- [ ] Free to try without a credit card, or the paywall is stated up front
- [ ] Site can take the traffic and the signup flow has been tested cold, today
- [ ] Blocked out the day to answer comments
- [ ] **Not** asking anyone for an upvote — anywhere, in any channel
