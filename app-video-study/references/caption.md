# Study video — writing the TikTok caption (@your-handle)

One job: stop the scroll with a true, surprising stat, then make people need the rest —
and end on the reflective turn that is this format's signature.

## Hard rules
- **Every number matches** `source.md` and what's on screen. Never round up for drama.
- **Front-load.** TikTok truncates after ~1 line in-feed — the first ~60 characters must
  carry the hook. Whole caption ~200–500 chars.
- **Voice:** curious, a little uncanny, credible. Not clinical, not hype, not advice.
- Name the **source once** (institution or journal + year). One `🌙`/`☾`/`🧠` is plenty.
- **No em dashes, no middle dots.** Don't use an em dash (—) or a middle-dot/bullet (• ·)
  anywhere in the caption. Rephrase with a comma, a colon, "or"/"vs", or a period.
- **No medical/financial advice.** Cultural + curiosity framing only.

## Structure (4 beats)
1. **Hook line** — the single most surprising figure, concrete. (~the video's `bignum`.)
2. **The context** — 1 sentence: who ran it, where/journal+year, and the effect
   (the comparison, e.g. "42% vs 17%").
3. **The turn** — the reflective "ask yourself" line from beat 6, in one sentence.
4. **CTA** — "Follow @your-handle for the science of …" + a curiosity nudge.

## Hashtags
5–8 on their own line: an evergreen core + 1–2 topical to the field.
Core: `#science #study #neuroscience #psychology #fyp`
Swap/add per topic: `#sleep #dreams #longevity #nutrition #memory #health` etc.

## Worked example (the dream-incubation video)
> Scientists played a sound while people slept, and 75% started dreaming about the exact
> puzzle it was tied to. 🌙
> In a 2026 Northwestern study, puzzles "cued" during REM got solved 42% of the time the
> next morning, vs 17% for the ones left out. One sleeper even asked a dream character for help.
> Your mind doesn't clock out when you do. So what problem would you hand to the night?
> Follow @your-handle for the science of the sleeping mind.
> #dreams #sleep #neuroscience #dreamscience #luciddreaming #sleepscience #psychology #fyp

## Where the facts come from
- Numbers → `source.md` (and they equal the on-screen `bignum` / `bars`).
- The turn → beat 6 (`ask-yourself`) of the video.
- Keep caption, on-screen text, and `source.md` in exact agreement.
